24. PURCHASABILITY RULE
==================================================

For PROD-00, a Product is purchasable only when:

Product.status == ACTIVE

AND

Shop.status == APPROVED

AND

Category.active == true

Stock availability is NOT decided by CatalogQueryService.

InventoryService owns stock locking/checking.

Therefore a stock=0 product may still resolve as a catalog ProductSnapshot,
and later InventoryService rejects insufficient stock.

Do not duplicate inventory responsibility in CatalogQueryService.
