## Answers to TD's six questions

1. **ADMIN-06 dependency: PASS.** OrderPlacementService obtains the effective CommissionQueryService policy and persists policy ID, rate and amount; amount is whole-VND HALF_UP on (subtotal - discount_total) * stored rate /100. Order captures these snapshots. The read side needs persisted snapshots, not Manager write limits or the current CommissionPolicy. Do not call requireEffectivePolicy while rendering historical revenue.
2. **Legacy: PASS on using valid stored snapshots with NULL policy ID, including legitimate zero amount.** commission_amount and commission_rate_snapshot are NOT NULL on the deployed schema; rate is constrained to [0,100], amount to [0,subtotal-discount_total]. policy ID is nullable, with FK when present. A NULL policy ID is NOT a missing snapshot marker. Zero is NOT missing; it may be zero rate, zero goods base or valid rounding. There is no provenance/completeness flag that can distinguish an imported placeholder 0 from a legitimate 0. Do not invent that distinction or recalculate from current policy. For real invalid/unknown imported facts (null after an unsupported import, nonfinite/out-of-range/fractional-invalid VND or missing required evidence), expose incomplete/unknown counts and commission/net totals as incomplete/unavailable; never COALESCE unknown to zero and present a complete result. Keep known zero as known. HP must approve any stronger historical provenance requirement; no backfill or ledger change for this read task.
3. **Refund timestamp: PASS.** Use immutable refunds.server_timestamp as the reporting event time. Label it Admin recording/completion time, NOT bank transfer time. Full COD refund subtracts persisted refund.amount and reverses the original Order commission_amount in this refund period, with no rewrite to sale period or policy snapshot. Do not compute commission reversal from refund.amount (which includes shipping), and do not emit new ledger writes.
4. **Return quantity: PASS under current full-return receipt contract.** received_at is the trusted RECEIVE_RETURN event, APPROVED with received_by/reference; vendor approval alone contributes no quantity reversal. Current ReturnRequest is unique per whole Order with no partial receipt/item quantity model. Therefore receipt subtracts all original OrderItem.quantity once at received_at. No restock or inventory query is involved. Missing/ambiguous receipt evidence must not imply a return quantity. Deduplicate at Order/receipt grain before joining items; do not subtract again on refund. This is not an extension to partial/failed-delivery returns.
5. **Timezone: PASS.** Explicit ZoneId Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh, injected Clock, compute reporting now once. Derive local day/month boundaries and convert both to UTC Instants; query [start,end). Use typed Instant/TIMESTAMPTZ parameters, not session timezone defaults or date(column) filters. Test month/year rollover and exact upper/lower bounds including UTC-adjacent events. Label timezone in UI.
6. **Aggregates/performance: PASS on separate event streams with grain discipline and EXPLAIN before indexes.** Actual SQL, completeness behavior and plans still need independent review. No speculative index/migration is required by this document.

## Event semantics and grain

- Sale stream: one Order event at persisted delivered_at; preserve original sale even when current status later becomes RETURN_REQUESTED/RETURNED/REFUNDED. Define trusted delivery/collection evidence and legacy exclusions explicitly. Do not filter historical sales to current status=DELIVERED only.
- Refund stream: one SUCCEEDED ledger row at server_timestamp, same-Order links; full refund amount and original commission reversal at this time. Payment PAID is historical collection evidence, not a reason to count cash twice.
- Return quantity stream: one full receipt event at received_at, then join original OrderItems for negative quantity per Product. Return and refund can belong to different periods. Do not use refund timestamp for top-product quantity or change original sale quantity.
- Money stays at Order/refund grain before any item join. SUM(DISTINCT amount) is NOT a fanout fix: two equal-value Orders must both count. Preaggregate payment evidence per Order and reject/flag ambiguous evidence, not a random MAX(PAID row). Top-product quantities aggregate separate positive delivery and negative receipt item streams with UNION ALL, then GROUP BY Product and stable ID tie-break/limit.
- HP has settled commission and net-quantity timing. UI must separately name buyer-paid total (grand_total, includes shipping), goods-after-discount, shipping, refund amount and commission. Do NOT silently label buyer-paid-minus-refund-minus-commission as Vendor earnings without an approved ownership policy for shipping fees. If DTO has a single revenue/net revenue metric, TD must state its exact formula to HP before SQL; QD does not invent a shipping-fee entitlement rule.
- Commission refund can occur with zero sale revenue in the current period, so period adjustments/net quantity/net totals may be negative. Do not clamp them to zero or allocate to original month. Show gross and adjustment components clearly. No period historical rewrite or new money ledger.

## Authorization and consistent reads

Only current persisted ACTIVE VENDOR and its approved owned Shop; server resolves Shop through the existing Shop boundary and identity service. No browser shop/actor/amount authority. Scope EVERY stream by that same trusted Shop, including linked refund/return rows. Use current approved ownership policy; no historical ownership transfer is inferred. Method and HTTP guard; foreign/locked/revoked/restricted Shop denied. Keep a consistent read-only snapshot across authorization and metrics (e.g. a fresh read-only REPEATABLE_READ service boundary from HTTP); document the snapshot authorization point. No write locks or Account-first lifecycle calls are needed for a read model. HP reviews any stronger mid-response revocation requirement.

Read-only bounded projections; constant query count independent of number of OrderItems (no per-Order/per-Product lazy fetch). Parameterized Shop/time bounds, stable limited top products. ## Follow-up: collection versus delivered sales — 2026-10-24

TD's proposal to expose two separate measures is technically consistent with the stored evidence. It does not settle which measure HP wants as the primary dashboard metric. QD does not approve a new Vendor earnings formula or shipping-fee entitlement through this review.

- **Collected from buyer:** successful persisted COD Payment amount at payments.paid_at. This is buyer cash collection including shipping, not money remitted to the Vendor. Require unambiguous successful collection evidence and the existing Order amount consistency; do not count a PAID row again after refund or use payment.created_at. Preserve the original collection in its period; display refund cash outflow separately at refunds.server_timestamp.
- **Delivered goods sales after discount:** persisted subtotal - discount_total at orders.delivered_at. Shipping is a separately labeled component and is excluded from this goods-sales measure. Keep historical delivered sales when the Order later enters Return/Refund. A delivery without valid delivery evidence must not be inferred from Payment alone.
- **Commission:** retain the previously approved snapshot timing: original commission in the delivery period and full reversal in the refund period. Introducing a collection view does not silently move commission recognition to paid_at.
- **Product quantity:** delivered quantities at delivered_at minus full received return quantities at received_at. A collection timestamp is not a substitute for receipt or delivery time.

Current CodPaymentService.collectCod is MANDATORY, requires Order SHIPPING, refreshes the single Payment under lock and records collection before the caller completes DELIVERED in the same fulfillment transaction. Consequently, paid_at and delivered_at can be close in ordinary COD data but represent different events. Do not assume equality in SQL or tests; use deterministic Clock-controlled boundary fixtures where timestamps straddle reporting boundaries. A transaction that rolls back contributes neither event.

If exposing a net collection or Vendor earnings card, HP must first approve the exact refund allocation, shipping handling and commission timing. A refund ledger amount includes shipping, so subtracting the entire refund.amount from a goods-only sales stream mixes bases. Show gross goods sales, shipping, cash collected, full cash refunds and commission adjustments independently until the net formula is settled. Do not reuse a single unlabeled revenue field for both measures.

Implementation acceptance adds: independent paid_at/delivered_at period fixtures, pending/unpaid exclusion, retained PAID historical collection after refund, no duplicate amount when joining items, and honest labels that do not imply payout/remittance. No SQL or production implementation is added by this follow-up. Await HP's primary label/formula decision, then QD reviews TD's actual aggregate SQL and PostgreSQL plans.
